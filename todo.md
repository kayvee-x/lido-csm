csm v2 will has 3 node Operator types

- Legecy EA (1.5e bond for first validator, 1.3e for subsequent ones, 3.5% for reward share)
- ICS (1.5e bond for first validator, 1.3e for subsequent ones, 6% reward share for first 16 keys and 3.5% for subsequent ones)
- permissionless operators (2.4e bond for first validator, 1.3e for subsequent ones, 3.5% for reward share)
